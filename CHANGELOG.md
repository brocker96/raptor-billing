# Changelog

## v0.1.0

First release of Raptor Billing.

- Shop and one-page checkout that any website can link to, the account is created on the way.
- Balance with auto-renewal from a saved card, payments through plata by mono.
- Client area: servers with live load, plan changes, invoices, balance and cards.
- Admin: revenue, clients, services, invoices, payments, plans, promo codes, settings.
- Automation: renewal invoices, reminders, suspension with a pay link, deletion, abandoned orders.
- Raptor Panel integration through the Raptor API, connected automatically by the installer.
- Installer for Ubuntu 24.04 and a built-in updater with backups and rollback.
